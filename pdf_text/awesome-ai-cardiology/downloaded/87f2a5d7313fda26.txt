                    2. BACKGROUND

Standard cardiac imaging techniques typically involve the ac-
quisition of multiple 2D slice stacks creating an anisotropic
3D volume which is HR in-plane, but LR in the through-plane
direction. Given an underlying unknown HR image yH , the
acquired LR image yL can be modelled as:
