Fig S1. Tables detailing architecture of CNNs used for Varying Resolution Study. Input resolution from top: 240x320,
120x160, 60x80, 30x40. Batch normalization and by ReLU applied after convolution (Conv) and fully connected (FC)
  layer. For each architecture, an additional fully connected layer with softmax activation outputs prediction over 15
    classes. Convolution layers have same padding and stride of 1. Pooling size of 2x2 used for Average and Max
                                                      Pooling layers.
