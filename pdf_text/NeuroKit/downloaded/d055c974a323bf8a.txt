Introduction

This paper consists of three parts, each devoted to a result due to J.-C. Yoccoz.
The proofs are all somewhat modiˇed, but they are also deeply in!uenced by
Yoccoz's work.
   The three results all bear on the local connectivity of Julia sets and bifurca-
tion sets for complex analytic polynomials. However, each is of great interest
in its own right and has many applications to other topics.
